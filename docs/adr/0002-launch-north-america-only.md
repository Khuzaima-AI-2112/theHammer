# Launch to North America only; exclude EU and UK organisations

theHammer's first ten paying Customers are targeted in North America. We decided
to make that an explicit contractual restriction rather than an accident of
sales: the terms state that the service is not offered to organisations
monitoring staff in the EU or the UK.

## Considered options

Building for EU/UK readiness at launch was rejected as premature. Employee
monitoring is among the hardest cases in EU and UK data protection law, it needs
qualified legal review that nobody has been engaged or paid to do, and no
Customer requires it yet.

## Consequences

North America is not a law-free zone — Canada has PIPEDA, Quebec has Law 25, and
California employee data has been in scope of CCPA/CPRA since 2023 — so the
Disclosure and retention work still has to be done properly. This decision buys
time, not exemption. Revisit before any EU or UK Customer is accepted, and treat
that as a funded piece of work with legal review, not a config change.
